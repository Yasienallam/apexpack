# Yasien Mohamed | Data & Operations Analytics Portfolio

**Two reproducible, recruiter-friendly analytics case studies** built with Python, pandas and Streamlit.

> **Data privacy:** This is the public, SAFE portfolio only. All Apex Pack examples here are **entirely fictional**. No actual business workbook, customer list, invoices, revenue figures, credentials, or confidential screenshots are published. Original business files are held separately in a private repository.

## Projects

### 1. [Apex Pack — Receivables & Profitability Analytics](apex_pack_demo/)
**Python · pandas · Streamlit · data quality**

A fictional-data demonstration of how an operational ledger can support cash-collection monitoring, product profitability analysis, and reconciliations. The real Apex Pack business exists, but **all example records in this public demonstration are invented**.

### 2. [BikeShare — Demand Forecasting](bikeshare_forecasting/)
**Python · scikit-learn · time series · reproducible evaluation**

Uses the public 2011–2012 [UCI Bike Sharing Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset) under CC BY 4.0. A one-day-ahead historical model uses prior rentals and known calendar fields only; validation and test are chronological, avoiding feature leakage.

The corresponding fully reproducible source package and the ATS-style resume are maintained separately.
